How it works

  • CSV and XLSX import with automatic and manual field mapping.
  • vCard 3.0 or 4.0 output, single file or split into chunks for picky importers.
  • Duplicate detection and merge on phone or email, with a problem-row report.
  • Phone cleanup keeps a leading plus and flags numbers that look too short or long.
